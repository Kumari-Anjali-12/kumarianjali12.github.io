Autonomous Robo-Car: Roborashtra 2023

**Competition:** PCCoE Roborashtra 2023

**Nature:** Team Project

Arduino UNO C/C++ (Embedded) Ultrasonic Sensors Hardware Interfacing

Project Summary

This project was the centerpiece of our entry into the **PCCoE Roborashtra 2023** competition. Our team designed, assembled, and programmed a mobile robot tasked with autonomously navigating a complex track while performing dynamic obstacle avoidance. This initiative was a deep dive into embedded systems, teamwork, and competitive control logic.

Key Contributions and Implementation

  • **Control Unit:** Utilized the **Arduino UNO** microcontroller for processing sensor input and executing motor commands in a time-critical environment.
  • **Sensing & Logic:** Integrated Ultrasonic Sensors (HC-SR04) to provide real-time distance measurements. I contributed significantly to implementing the **Obstacle Avoidance Logic** in C/C++, focusing on smooth deceleration and optimal re-routing paths.
  • **Teamwork & Testing:** Coordinated rigorous field testing sessions with the team, iterating on the physical build and tuning the PID-like parameters in the software for peak competitive performance.

Competition Outcome & Evidence

While the focus was on the engineering challenge, the project's success is documented through competition recognition and visual assets.
